# Public Brand Discovery Report

Date: 2026-07-16. Status: discovery only; no rename is approved or implemented.

## Product identity

The product is an enterprise developer platform that unifies safe project bootstrap, engineering standards, documentation infrastructure, AI providers, integrations, templates, plugins, release engineering, deployment preparation, and developer experience. Its durable position is **the governed foundation for creating and operating modern software projects**, not a single AI feature or CLI.

Primary users are platform engineers, staff engineers, DevOps/release teams, open-source maintainers, AI application teams, and organizations standardizing project delivery. The brand must support an open-source project, commercial distribution, and a broader product family over a ten-year horizon.

## Naming directions

1. **Foundation and terrain** — stable ground on which teams build.
2. **Orchestration and flow** — coordinated systems without implying one tool.
3. **Craft and precision** — disciplined engineering and repeatability.
4. **Momentum and navigation** — moving projects safely from idea to production.

## Availability methodology and limits

The review used exact-name indexed web, GitHub, domain-string, company, and public trademark searches on 2026-07-16. “No prominent exact hit” is only a discovery signal: it does not prove a GitHub handle, domain, company name, or trademark is legally available. A registrar check, GitHub reservation, and professional multi-jurisdiction trademark clearance are mandatory before approval.

Known conflicts demonstrate why this gate matters: Aveniq identifies itself as a protected/registered technology brand; Nexora has active software-related uses and trademark filings; Orqestra is an existing technology platform; Novaryn is used by multiple software businesses; and Veylora has a live/pending US mark and active commerce. See [Aveniq legal notice](https://aveniq.ch/legal-information-en), [Nexora trademark record](https://uspto.report/TM/99203230), [Orqestra terms](https://dashboard.orqestra.io/terms-and-conditions.pdf), [Novaryn software studio](https://www.novaryn.io/), and [Veylora trademark record](https://trademarks.justia.com/997/28/veylora-99728085.html).

## Superseded Recommendation: Teravyn

**Teravyn** is the strongest current candidate. It is a single, pronounceable coined word; sounds stable rather than trend-bound; does not constrain the platform to AI, a CLI, or project generation; and supports product extensions such as Teravyn Cloud, Teravyn Enterprise, Teravyn Registry, and Teravyn Connect without semantic strain.

Compared with Kordexa, it has no exact company-registry signal in this pass. Compared with Calyrix and Qorven, it is easier to hear and spell globally. Compared with the blocked names, it currently has a substantially cleaner indexed collision signal. Its “terrain/foundation plus evolution” story matches the product’s durable role better than names centered only on orchestration or speed.

This recommendation was rejected after later research found active technology and software businesses using Teravyn. The approved compatibility-release identity is **Forgevena**, with the caption **“Governed engineering from idea to production.”** npm returned `E404` for the exact `forgevena` package identifier on 2026-07-16, but registry availability is not legal clearance. Professional trademark and jurisdiction-specific review remain required before broad commercial launch.
