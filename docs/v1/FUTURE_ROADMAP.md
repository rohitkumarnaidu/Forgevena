# Future Roadmap

Maintain stability first. The canonical plan is the [Forgevena Versioned Product and Innovation Roadmap](../strategy/FORGEVENA_VERSIONED_PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md).

Near-term work completes `v1.3` reliability and security evidence before provider, plugin, MCP, template, governance, observability, workflow, or intelligence claims expand. A cross-version [Enterprise Update Management Strategy](../strategy/ENTERPRISE_UPDATE_MANAGEMENT.md) connects those component lifecycles through trusted discovery, compatibility and policy resolution, installation-channel awareness, explicit consent, verification, health checks, history, offline bundles, and recovery.

Priorities remain issue-driven compatibility fixes, secure account-backed integration testing, documentation and community improvements, measured performance regression prevention, and evidence-based developer experience enhancements. No new platform layer is planned without demonstrated need and an approved ADR.
